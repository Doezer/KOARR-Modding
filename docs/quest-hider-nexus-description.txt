[b]Quest Hider[/b]

A cleaner journal. Your quests, your choice.

Keep the quests you want to see and hide the ones you want to leave for later. Restore them whenever you like, without abandoning them or changing their progress.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Hide selected ongoing quests from the quest journal.[/*]
[*]Switch to a dedicated hidden-quest list to restore them.[/*]
[*]Filter hidden quests' objective icons on the world map, local map and minimap.[/*]
[*]Keep your quest progress and tracked quest selection.[/*]
[*]Save your hidden-quest preferences separately from game saves.[/*]
[*]See the keyboard controls in the journal's reminder area.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]

Windows PC, GOG Kingdoms of Amalur: Re-Reckoning with Fatesworn, and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomsofamalurrereckoning/mods/9]Mod Framework v1.4 by DevCortez[/url]. Install the framework separately using its author's instructions.

This version supports the tested GOG executable. Steam, Epic and other executable builds are not supported by this release; the native helper disables itself on an unsupported build.

All Quest Markers is optional. Quest Hider installs independently.

[b]Installation[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game.[/*]
[*]Extract the complete Quest Hider ZIP into the game folder containing koa.exe, merging the mods folder.[/*]
[*]Start the game, load your save and open the quest journal.[/*]
[/list]

No console command is needed. Map filtering becomes active after the first time you open the quest journal in each session.

[b]How to use[/b]
[list]
[*]Select an ongoing quest row and press [b]F6[/b] to hide it.[/*]
[*]Press [b]F7[/b] to switch between your normal journal and hidden quests.[/*]
[*]Select a quest in the hidden list and press [b]F6[/b] to restore it.[/*]
[/list]

Expand a category to see its quests. Hidden rows have a [Hidden] prefix. Closing and reopening the journal returns to the normal list. Empty category headers can remain visible.

Controls use the keyboard even when navigating the journal with a controller. You can change the keys in mods/QuestHider/config.lua with the game closed.

[b]What hiding does[/b]

Hiding changes visibility, not quest state. Hidden quests can still progress and complete. Your tracked quest remains selected internally; its HUD text and overhead billboards remain visible.

The map filter concerns objective icons. Quest-giver icons, fast-travel points and other points of interest are unchanged. Shared objectives remain visible when another visible quest owns them. Icons with unresolved ownership also remain visible.

Completed quests cannot be newly hidden. A previously hidden quest can still be restored after completion.

[b]Preferences[/b]

Hidden quests are remembered in mods/QuestHider/profiles/, outside your game saves. Preferences follow the character name across saves. [b]Characters with identical names share preferences.[/b] Set a unique profile string in config.lua if you need to separate them.

When upgrading from a preview, replace the DLL and Lua files together, preserve the profiles folder and carry any custom settings into the new config.lua.

[b]Compatibility and testing[/b]

Journal hiding, hidden-list switching and restoration have been confirmed in-game on the supported GOG build. Map filtering and persistence are implemented, but broader testing across quests, full restarts, DLC and other UI mods is ongoing.

All Quest Markers and Quest Hider use separate files and renderer hooks. Compatibility with every other mod is not claimed. If something goes wrong, include the affected quest and the [QuestHider] messages from your framework log when reporting it.

[b]Uninstall or reset[/b]

Close the game. Remove mods/quest_hider.json, mods/quest_hider_native.dll and mods/QuestHider/, then restart. Leave the framework and other mods in place.

[b]Remove both the JSON and DLL when disabling the mod.[/b] The framework loads native DLLs even when their JSON file is absent.

To reset only hidden-quest preferences, delete mods/QuestHider/profiles/ with the game closed.
